What is the difference between Sales and Profit : 
Sales represent the revenue generated from selling products, while Profit represents the amount remaining after deducting the associated costs.

Why might a product have high sales but low profit?
A product may have high sales but low profit because of high discounts, product costs, shipping expenses, and low profit margins.

Why does the company need to track shipping dates?
Tracking order dates and shipping dates helps the company measure delivery delays, monitor shipping performance, improve customer satisfaction, and optimize delivery operations.
